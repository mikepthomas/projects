# Table of contents

# Intro

I have created this page to pull together terrain from multiple sources that can be used to create scenes from popular places in the Fallout universe to be used as terrain for Fallout Wasteland Warfare games.

# Digital Taxidermy

## 3D Printed

Digital Taxidermy have created a fair few [Sci-fi Spool Tower](printer-spool-tower#sci-fi) creations that could be used for terrain for `Fallout Wasteland Warfare`.

They also have a [Wild West Bundle](https://cults3d.com/en/users/digital_taxidermy/bundles/wild-west-bundle) available that has items that could be used for `Dry Rock Gulch` or `New Vegas` style Terrain.

# Modiphius

## Print at Home

Much of the Resin terrain that was released by Modiphius, is also available to puchase as 3D Printable STLs.

As there are a fair few of these STL packs, I collated these into separate pages depending on their theme:

- [Brotherhood of Steel Encampment](/projects/brotherhood-of-steel)
- [Nuka-World](/projects/nuka-world)
- [Red Rocket Truck Stop](/projects/red-rocket)
- [Sanctuary Hills](/projects/sanctuary-hills)
- [Vault-Tec Vault](/projects/vault)

Others that do not fit into the above themes can be found below.
